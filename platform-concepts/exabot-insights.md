# Exabot Insights

This document provides details on how Exaforce platform’s Exabot Insights can solve the challenges in obtaining reliable outcomes in obtaining insights and establishing a “readiness” state in responding and handling security threats and risks for your organization

The Exaforce platform can seamlessly integrate your environment and read logs, and then continuously generates meaningful insights that are not only based on historical data, but also account for the runtime/current events. Integrating your environment data as a “data source” with agent-driven Exaforce platform nullifies your need for integrating variety of external security monitoring tools while obtaining reliable and meaningful insights at a much efficient and faster rate.

**Note:** You must integrate your environment (referred to as _data source_ in this document) first to obtain insights for your environment. You can integrate more than one data source.

The **Exabot Insights** is a capability that provides you with your overall security, risk, and threat posture in terms of overall trends for identities across different data sources and also overall readiness of your organization for threat/risk handling. The Exabot Insights bot presents rich visualization and fine-grained controls to filter insights as per your needs.

The **Exabot Insights** offers the following services for you, on a per data source basis or across all data sources:

### **Identity Insights**

Presents trends for all your identities across your data sources in terms of both human and machine identity usage, and offers various visualizations for the trends. and shows how these different types of identities interact with your applications. The insights bot present the following insights derived out of historic as well as runtime dats:

* Overall security score (an average of risk and threat scores for all your identities)
* Identities with top risks, and top threats
* Privilege escalations
* Unused identities
* Top and least IDP app usage trends
* Identities by location

The identity insights bot offers all of the above and more, with a wide range of visualizations that also offer extensive native (scoped to a section) and cross filtering options (scoped to entire page). Also, the insights page presents options to directly view detailed investigate page for a specific identity or a specific risk or threat page associated with an identity.

### Readiness

Readiness represents Exaforce’s evaluation of your organization’s security posture in terms of risks, threats, and possible coverage analysis for potential compromises. The readiness evaluation consists of evaluation of your log (raw data) coverage, health of the log sources, and any critical activities that occured in the past 24 hours. The readiness evaluation also offers a MITRE ATT\&CK (Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge) Matrix and coverage report for your various data sources for the past 24 hours. Every day the readiness evaluation is refreshed, while also presenting emerging patterns. The Exaforce readiness also offers you option to obtain insights into specific data source or across all data sources, using filtering options.
